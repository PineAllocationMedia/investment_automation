**Version:** 1.0 — Proposed strategic design

**Scope:** PAM Financial Systems only  
**Status:** `DISCOVERY_COMPLETE / BLUEPRINT_PROPOSED / NOT_IMPLEMENTED`  
**Authority:** CEO-selected design direction from 36 sequential decisions; **not** authorization to amend CEO-protected files, change canonical branches, deploy, promote V2, operate automation, delete files, move data, or execute investment transactions.  
**Repository:** `PineAllocationMedia/investment_automation`  
**Repository reference inspected:** `model-v2/expected-return-challenger` at `767890eaafccd1ae5a6f457dfdb9bb3796ee6c1b` (live GitHub observation, 2026-10-27). `main` remains the repository default branch and V1's frozen operational baseline. Repository state may subsequently change.

> **Strategic thesis.** PAM Financial Systems should become an AI-native, adaptable, evidence-disciplined investment intelligence system with three integrated ChatGPT Projects, excellent versioned prompts, minimal required code, Blossom-centered portfolio analysis, and a governed GitHub institutional memory. Its long-term advantage is the quality of its research process, context awareness, accumulated analytical history, and disciplined learning—not a sprawling proprietary parser stack or automatic trading. The human retains investment and governance authority.

## 1. Executive decision and scope

### 1.1 The proposed 180-degree reform

Move from a software-centric development program toward an **AI-first operating model**. Specialist AI conducts most company research, financial interpretation, valuation, investment comparison, portfolio reasoning and report writing through carefully engineered prompts. Use deterministic calculations, structured records or targeted code only when they demonstrably improve accuracy, reproducibility, privacy or usability. Do not preserve technical complexity merely because it already exists.

The initial organization is deliberately small:

1. **PAM Financial Systems — Command Center**: understands requests, selects approved strategy and relevant expertise, assembles concise research mandates, integrates outputs, applies reporting and archive rules, and prepares proposed improvements.
2. **PAM Investment Research**: conducts fundamental and qualitative research, Quality, Durability, Expected Return, Confidence, valuation, macro/sector context, company comparison, and opportunity discovery.
3. **PAM Portfolio Intelligence**: interprets supported Blossom portfolio visuals, evaluates Portfolio Room, position lifecycle and transaction economics, and proposes contribution weights or exceptional trims.

These are **ChatGPT Projects/workspaces**, not independently executing autonomous agents and not replacements for PAM's constitutional specialist charters. The existing Program Manager / Chief of Staff continues to coordinate the wider PAM organization.

### 1.2 Target experience

- **On demand first:** a person asks a concise question, supplies or selects an editable investment-request template, and receives useful, appropriately qualified research.
- **Low-friction research:** the Command Center designs or drafts the necessary specialist assignments. Routine work should ideally stay within the Command Center; handoffs are short, explicit and justified.
- **Blossom-centered portfolio work:** for a requested portfolio-management analysis, supported portfolio visuals supply observable holdings and weights; separate financial research supplies issuer and investment context. No assumption that Blossom visuals expose every cost, account constraint or lot.
- **Contribution-centered recommendations:** primarily allocate *new* capital according to the investor's mandate; positions are trimmed or liquidated exceptionally and only when justified by evidence and after-cost economics.
- **Short decision output:** final user-facing investment report is intended to take **10 minutes or less** to read; a detailed evidence dossier can exist separately.
- **Efficient routine:** targeted weekly intelligence plus on-demand research; target **roughly one hour of ordinary human involvement per week** after stabilization. Exceptional deep-dive research and setup may take longer.
- **Cloud first:** GitHub for governed knowledge; ChatGPT for reasoning; compatible cloud documents for editable request templates and confidential supporting records; no terminal or local runtime by default. A designated PC is an exceptional technical workstation, not a permanent dependency.

### 1.3 Explicit non-goals and exclusions

This Blueprint does **not** authorize brokerage execution, automatic trading, market timing, silent policy tuning, financial commercialization, new data subscriptions, source-rights violations, public disclosure of private holdings, unattended Codex orchestration, any change to the Blossom media mandate, or premature elimination of V1/V2. It does not assert that prompt engineering alone creates a durable commercial moat, that LLMs cannot hallucinate, or that browser-based access to a specific Blossom account is already functional.

## 3. Target functional architecture

### 3.1 Separation of responsibilities

**Command Center (CC).** Interprets the user request; identifies goal, horizon, style, portfolio scope, broker/account economics, output depth and source date; identifies missing *material* information; selects relevant methodologies; drafts an execution plan; conducts ordinary synthesis directly where appropriate; routes complex tasks; checks handoff coverage; enforces the main report standard; proposes, but does not unilaterally canonize, research-memory records. It may question inappropriate assumptions; it does not modify the user's investment mandate in silence.

**Investment Research (IR).** Owns company and security research, business economics, financial statements, industry structure, competitive advantages, Quality, Durability, Expected Return, Confidence, bear/base/bull assumptions, macro and sector context, opportunity discovery and ranking support. It separates documented company facts from analyst assumptions and forecasts. It checks material arithmetic independently using available tools when feasible. It does not propose executable account-level weights without verified portfolio context.

**Portfolio Intelligence (PI).** Owns the interpretation of available Blossom portfolio observations, date/time of observation, weights where visible, account/broker input completeness, shared economic exposure, Portfolio Room, contribution sizing, lifecycle states, net transaction economics and exceptional sell analysis. It must state which portfolio facts were actually observed and which were not; do not infer precise holdings from graphic proportions alone. It cannot place trades or access private third-party accounts without explicit permissions.

**Independent assurance.** Not a fourth Project initially. Risk-tiered verification may use a separate fresh-context review, source-checking tools or an existing authorized Architecture & Audit / Data & Evidence review where relevant. A second model agreeing with the first does not itself prove source correctness.

### 3.2 Invocation tiers

1. **Simple:** CC answers directly using a preapproved method and light evidence checks. No needless specialist handoff.
2. **Standard:** CC issues one compact research request to IR or PI and integrates the result; this is the default meaningful research mode.
3. **Consequential/complex:** CC uses both expert Projects and, when justified, independent challenge of assumptions, arithmetic and source claims. Minimize turns and preserve the exact evidence chain.
4. **Unsupported:** When access, source, or data is insufficient, CC completes supportable partial analysis, flags unsupported conclusions, and asks for at most the decisive missing information rather than automatically abandoning the entire task.

ChatGPT Projects are context containers, not a native inter-Project message queue. Automation claims must be established by actual supported capabilities and observed tests. Fallback design: one copy-ready handoff package per necessary transition, ideally no repeated full prompt preambles.

### 3.3 Universal foundation and strategy modules

The five V2 lenses—**Quality, Durability, Expected Return, Confidence, Portfolio Room**—become a shared *analytical vocabulary* rather than a claim that the same numerical weights or valuation metrics work for every security or investor objective.

Potential strategy modules include High-CAGR Quality Compounder (initially closest to validated V2 philosophy), Capital Preservation, Balanced, Income, and other separately specified mandates. Each module defines its eligible instruments, horizon, loss constraints, benchmark/opportunity-cost reference, risk language, rebalancing or sales doctrine, and what minimum evidence is decision-grade. No strategy may silently override the chosen mandate. New strategy policies require CEO decision before becoming authoritative.

## 4. Proposed prompt architecture and editable request model

### 4.1 Four layers, not enormous pasted prompts

| Layer | Purpose | Proposed authority/storage |
|---|---|---|
| **L0 — Constitutional safeguards** | Human control, evidence integrity, prohibited actions, source rights, privacy, no self-modification | Governed GitHub documents; CEO-protected changes require approval |
| **L1 — Specialist method** | Project charter, task boundaries, five pillars, risk-specific standards, output contract | Versioned GitHub prompt/methodology files; synchronized into actual Project instructions/sources with tracked version IDs |
| **L2 — Request envelope** | Investor goal, horizon, requested portfolio, funds, broker/account specifics, material constraints | Short editable template; Google Docs may be an editing surface, but approved version/rules remain GitHub-referenced |
| **L3 — Task-specific assignment** | What to research, source cut-off, scope, method, acceptance criteria, report budget, handoff destination | CC-generated prompt for one analysis; archived by stable analysis ID for consequential work |

**Important:** A bare GitHub or Google Doc hyperlink does not guarantee automatic retrieval, full context or version correctness. At each handoff, the receiving Project should know the precise applicable method version and have access to required context. If lookup fails, use a small required excerpt or report the missing method, not silent improvisation. Project memory is useful contextual recall but **not** canonical version-controlled storage.

### 4.2 Minimum request envelope (illustrative, editable)

```yaml
request_id: PAM-RESEARCH-YYYYMMDD-NNN
as_of: YYYY-MM-DD
request_type: company_research | candidate_comparison | portfolio_contribution | monitoring
investment_goal: high_cagr | capital_preservation | balanced | other_explicit
horizon: user_specified
risk_constraints: user_specified
portfolio_source: none | blossom_screenshot | authorized_blossom_web_view
portfolio_observation_time: recorded_or_unknown
contribution_amount_and_currency: optional
account_type: optional
broker_platform: optional
trading_fee_and_fx_terms: supplied_or_unverified
permitted_sources_and_budget: official_and_allowed_free_sources
research_depth: routine | standard | consequential
output: main_report_under_10_min + evidence_appendix_if_needed
human_authority: no_trade; no_mandate_change; no_repo_merge
```

This is a **proposed schema**, not an implemented contract. Omit or explicitly mark unavailable fields; do not infer an account type, fee schedule, investment risk tolerance or exact positions.

### 4.3 Compact specialist handoff contract

Each necessary handoff should contain: `ANALYSIS_ID`, `PROMPT_VERSION`, `METHOD_VERSION`, `OBJECTIVE`, `AS_OF`, `SOURCE_REFERENCES`, `FACTS_VERIFIED`, `INTERPRETATIONS`, `ASSUMPTIONS`, `UNKNOWN_OR_DISPUTED`, `SCENARIOS`, `IMPACT_ON_DECISION`, `CONFIDENCE_RATIONALE`, `FURTHER_CHECK_REQUIRED`, `NEXT_OWNER` and `OUTPUT_BUDGET`. The source archive or secure locator must support the material facts. Other fields are optional when immaterial. The Command Center should reject handoffs that conceal important missing facts rather than blindly concatenate persuasive prose.

### 4.4 Prompt-improvement governance

Maintain version IDs, change rationale, test cases, observed errors, cross-case regression notes and review status. A model can **propose** a change, but must not quietly update authoritative investment policy, change thresholds after seeing disappointing outputs, or overwrite prior historical prompt versions. Favor reducing prompt length without deleting indispensable safeguards. Do not overload each Project with an entire monolithic repository history; retrieve the minimum relevant evidence and contracts.

## 5. Financial intelligence methodology

### 5.1 Evidence hierarchy and disciplined usefulness

**Source priority for material financial claims:** original filings/official company statements/regulatory data where available; reputable third-party financial data and expert sources for discovery, context and cross-checks; clearly marked analyst or AI estimates for forecasting. The actual authority depends on metric, country, as-of date and permitted use. Use several sources when a material conflict needs resolving; do not assume web popularity makes an observation correct.

**Two research confidence levels:**

- **Exploratory:** partial facts, qualitative judgments, identifiable estimates and scenarios are allowed; the conclusion must disclose uncertainty and not pretend to be executable advice.
- **Decision-grade:** material portfolio and valuation conclusions require a sufficiency check on source lineage, security identity, period/currency/unit, relevant adjustments, arithmetic, portfolio facts and key assumptions. If a decisive input is missing, provide a conditional recommendation or withhold only that unsupported conclusion.

This is not a blanket license to guess financial facts. **An observed figure, a source interpretation, an analyst assumption and a model forecast must be distinguishable.** The principle is: *do as much useful defensible analysis as possible; stop unsupported inferences, not the whole investigation.*

### 5.2 Temporal integrity

Preserve the difference between information captured today and information genuinely accessible on a prior date. Every consequential analysis needs an `as_of` date and source observation/capture date. Historical simulations must use genuinely point-in-time-qualified evidence or be labeled retrospective diagnostics, never hindsight-free backtests merely because prompts say so. Subsequent outcome checks must not revise what earlier researchers allegedly knew.

### 5.3 Valuation and ranking

For long-term High CAGR, keep useful V2 principles: normalized earnings/free cash flow or owner economics, distribution policy, economically defensible growth, valuation normalization, bear/base/bull scenarios, horizon approximately 5–10 years where mandate-appropriate, explicit confidence, and opportunity cost versus a suitable benchmark (VFV for the established Canadian High-CAGR context unless separately changed). Alternative asset types and short-term mandates may require different methods.

AI may choose earnings, FCF, DCF, relative multiples, asset value or other recognized approaches when supported by the business and available evidence. Explain why the methodology fits and identify unreliable or unsuitable methods. Use calculations with units and transparent assumptions; check significant arithmetic with a calculator or deterministic tool where available. A precise expected-CAGR figure is *not obligatory* when its assumptions cannot be supported.

Candidate selection is multi-stage: existing holdings → relevant watchlist → wider discovery if incremental potential justifies time → shortlist deep dive → compare expected return, quality, durability, confidence and portfolio room → after-cost contribution recommendation. Prefer strong candidates over a superficial universal sweep. A benchmark, a qualified alternative or a conditional non-commitment remains possible when no individual stock clears the strategy hurdle.

### 5.4 Macro and risk

Macroeconomics, geopolitical developments, liquidity and sector changes inform company earnings resilience, valuation discount rates, competitive durability and portfolio overlap. **No market-timing engine:** do not change cash allocation, sell or rotate merely due to recession headlines, volatility or speculative regime labels. Differences between approved mandates remain explicit.

### 5.5 Portfolio management from Blossom

For portfolio decisions, the primary input is **what is observable on the authorized Blossom account view or user-supplied Blossom screenshots**; public investor portfolios are research context, not verified private portfolios. A visual record should carry observation date, source/visibility scope, interpreted holdings and weight confidence, unknown account constraints, and any inaccessible sections. Public profiles may expose percentages rather than detailed account data; never equate them with private position statements.

The system should primarily recommend the **percentage allocation of the next contribution** across the strongest suitably researched candidates. It can propose paused buys, holds and exceptional trims/sales. Position lifecycle states include thesis intact, uncertainty increasing, material deterioration, structural thesis break, concentration or other mandate-specific risk. Price decline is not automatically thesis failure. Evaluate sales using expected incremental value *net of* transaction commissions, FX conversion, taxes where material and knowable, currency balances and account restrictions. When exact broker fees, account type or tax lot are not provided, label the transaction economics conditional, not invented.

No direct broker actions, order entry, discretionary portfolio management or implied securities execution authority. Every allocation is an analytical recommendation for the human.

## 6. Decision reports and research history

### 6.1 Reader experience

A normal user-facing decision report should take **10 minutes or less** to read. Suggested hierarchy:

1. **Decision line:** answer the request immediately; include whether it is a recommendation, an exploratory view or conditional research.
2. **Investment context:** strategy and horizon, source/portfolio as-of date, relevant account type and currency.
3. **Key candidate comparison:** short ordered table of opportunity, core rationale, expected-return *range or qualitative standing*, confidence and key uncertainty.
4. **Proposed action where requested:** contribution percentages summing to 100% of the specified contribution, with eligible benchmark/fallback and explicit rounding; or why allocation is withheld/conditional.
5. **Main reasons and counterarguments:** what drives the outcome, material downside and thesis-break conditions.
6. **Evidence status:** material facts, estimates, missing data, source links, and reference to deeper dossier.

Do not fill the entire 10-minute limit by default. A weekly digest may be two minutes; a complex research brief may use the full allowance. Full evidence dossiers can be longer but should not dilute the main decision report.

### 6.2 Dated research records

For consequential analyses, preserve a compact append-only record containing `ANALYSIS_ID`, date and time, requested mandate, project/prompt/method versions, financial data snapshot/citations, relevant portfolio observation, key facts, assumptions, uncertainty, recommended actions, human response where voluntarily recorded, and later follow-up IDs. Prevent retrospective editing of original conclusions; append corrections or reassessments with their own identities.

The record should make these questions answerable: **What was known at the time? What did PAM conclude? What evidence supported that conclusion? What changed? Was a later discrepancy caused by source error, modeling assumption, overlooked event, ordinary market variance or hindsight bias?** A strong process may lose money; a weak process may appear right by luck.

### 6.3 Evidence-based prompt evolution

Maintain selected benchmark cases spanning varied issuers, periods, data availability, currencies, reporting standards, security types and investment mandates. A proposed prompt update must be checked against those cases and at least one not-used-to-design-it case when possible. Score factual accuracy, important omission rate, arithmetic correctness, source-date integrity, caveat quality, recommendation consistency, excessive abstention and human workload. Approval changes method versions; it does not rewrite historic analyses.

## 7. Cloud-based operations and realistic capability limits

### 7.1 Three environments do not imply three autonomous workers

ChatGPT Projects group project instructions, chats and accessible resources; they are not guaranteed to exchange messages or inherit each other's memory. Project instructions must stay concise and referenced to versioned GitHub methods. A central Work-mode task may be able to interact with supported connected tools or browsers, but this must be observed for each requested integration. Do **not** design the MVP assuming a scheduling feature routes tasks between Projects, runs arbitrary chains, or automatically commits GitHub research records.

**Fallback:** CC performs ordinary work centrally; for difficult research it prepares one standardized message to paste into IR and optionally one to PI; results return through short packets. Record manual time and error rate. Prefer actual supported Work actions over building custom orchestration; never restart paused unattended Codex-development orchestration by calling it "research coordination." Any genuinely new unattended infrastructure requires separate approval and risk review.

### 7.2 Scheduled Tasks and weekly intelligence

Potential later tasks: a short weekly public-market briefing, selected event alerts, and a reminder for periodic research evaluation. Tasks must have allowed sources, explicit schedule/timezone, notification destination, no spending/transaction authority, and a human review path. **Current product constraint:** OpenAI documents that a Scheduled Task created in a Project cannot access files uploaded to or stored in that Project; thus a task may need separately accessible approved sources. No task should be installed merely because this Blueprint describes it.

Proposed steady-state workload target (illustrative, not observed): review weekly summary 10–15 minutes; assess consequential alerts and decide on extra research 10–15 minutes; contribution or portfolio analysis when due 20–30 minutes; review a compact memory/update queue 5–10 minutes. Weekly time varies with contribution cycle; baseline by stopwatch during trials. If tasks generate alerts that increase decision burden without measurable value, reduce cadence.

### 7.3 Blossom and Work compatibility

Blossom is the preferred **visual portfolio input**, not a source of automatically verified positions or broker statements. Before promising autonomous portfolio reading, test read-only website compatibility, sign-in experience, exact private/public view access, data completeness, permission limitations and terms of use. Enter credentials only in an authorized browser takeover, never in prompts or the repository. Avoid exposing unnecessary private investor or family data. Public holdings of other investors are for research only, subject to permitted-use and attribution constraints.

If Work cannot reliably view Blossom, use screenshots the user supplies (with dates and explicit visibility constraints). The first prototype may prove the **three-Project workflow** via screenshots without proving browser automation; milestone records must distinguish these facts.

## 8. Institutional memory: GitHub brain without archive clutter

### 8.1 Three storage tiers

**Tier I — Governed institutional memory (GitHub).** Project constitution/strategy/governance, Work Orders, role charters, approved strategy modules, instructions, versioned prompt library, evidence contracts, decision registry, prompt evaluation suites, research methodologies, source-rights rules, historical lessons and concise technical retrospectives. Treat this as the canonical *knowledge* layer, not automatically an operational truth for every unsynced ChatGPT Project.

**Tier II — Traceable research history (GitHub or controlled archive with index).** Selected dated analyses, source references/hashes, prompt and method versions, valuation assumptions, uncertainty, recommendation outcomes and retrospective evaluations. Where the record or rights are inappropriate for the repo, preserve a secure locator and integrity metadata. Git history is not a substitute for immutable original-source preservation where needed.

**Tier III — Private/sensitive records (protected storage).** Blossom screenshots with account details, private holdings, statements, cost bases, tax information, credentials (which should not be archived in plaintext anywhere), large source archives and regulated/licensed material. Apply least privilege, secure retention, deletion controls, and a strict separation from PAM Media publication data. Do not use private portfolio data to create public Blossom content without a separate explicit decision.

### 8.3 What the three-computer audit must capture

**Discovery scope:** each authorized Mac, PC/WSL and Chromebook workspace; obvious PAM-related files plus bounded searches for local repositories, uncommitted changes, branch refs, test fixtures, terminal scripts, downloads, exported ZIPs, notes, captured sources, logs, work orders and experimental runtime artifacts. **Preservation order:** inventory read-only → cross-check against remote canonical repo, local history and approved archive → verify unique material and usable restoration → propose target location → separately approve any transfer or repository PR → only after verified preservation propose cleanup → separate deletion authorization. "Clean" means organized and verified—not a demand to uninstall existing system components without a migration plan.

## 9. Validation framework and separate readiness gates

### 9.1 Unit of proof and baseline

The minimum evaluation unit is a **versioned analysis run**: dated request, exact prompt/method IDs, available source evidence, evaluation context, model/tool configuration when known, resulting claims/calculations and reviewer observations. Real filings and dated source records are preferred; synthetic tests are useful for checking mechanisms but do not establish real-world source authority. V1 and V2 can be shadow comparators where they answer comparable questions, but agreement with them alone is not proof.

### 9.2 Research-ready

Demonstrate several diversified, genuinely source-grounded issuer/security cases using the three-Project operating design as needed. Verify critical source values, periods, units, security identification, important calculations, source rights, missing-data behavior and reasonable valuation uncertainty. Research-ready permits **human-reviewed research** for the supported case types; it is not a production recommendation-engine promotion.

### 9.3 Portfolio-ready

Using an authorized Blossom visual source or dated user-provided screenshot, demonstrate reliable visible-holdings transcription, known/unknown discrimination, concentration interpretation, candidate comparisons, example contribution weights and after-cost implications with explicitly supplied fee/account data. Include adversarial tests for cropped views, duplicate tickers, stale snapshots, omitted cash, currency ambiguity, invisible account holdings and public-versus-private portfolio scope. A screenshot demonstration is not a verified Work-to-Blossom browser integration.

### 9.4 Routine-ready

Demonstrate a weekly digest from permitted and auditable sources, requested deeper analysis, stable reporting, readable output, source references, proper versioning and a low-friction archival queue. Measure median and outlier human interaction time across real weekly cycles; the **~1 hour/week** target must be tested rather than asserted. No scheduled monitoring should become authoritative or routinely active without separate approval.

### 9.5 Promotion/retirement gate is separate

Neither Research-ready, Portfolio-ready nor Routine-ready automatically alters V1 production, V2 challenger, branch roles, model fingerprints, or shadow/promotion criteria. Any production replacement or archival retirement needs an independent technical and financial evidence assessment, explicit CEO decision, rollback plan and consideration of historical comparison integrity. Do not infer investment alpha from a profitable backtest or a small set of favorable cases.

### 9.6 Proposed acceptance scorecard (targets, not evidence)

| Dimension | What to measure | Proposed pass/fail logic |
|---|---|---|
| Factual integrity | Material quoted financial facts vs original records | No unresolved material misstatement in a gated case; unsupported fields labeled |
| Numerical integrity | Financial calculations, return scenarios, weights | Material arithmetic cross-checked; percentages reconcile; assumptions visible |
| Temporal integrity | As-of/capture dates, no hindsight | Every consequential case labeled; retrospective knowledge never disguised as PIT |
| Analytical usefulness | Does it answer the user's investment question? | Human reviewer finds decision-support value without unsupported certainty |
| Risk discipline | Portfolio/mandate limits, costs, exception sales | No silent mandate breach; documented costs and conditions |
| Source rights/privacy | Licensed use and private records | No unauthorized sharing or unsafe credential storage |
| Workload | Human minutes and transfers | Measure; goal ~60 minutes weekly for routine operation after stabilization |
| Report UX | Readability and key risks | Standard decision report can be read in ≤10 minutes |
| Traceability | Replay/reference of methods and evidence | Dated research can be understood without reconstructing the entire chat |

Numerical thresholds beyond these proposed safeguards must be selected after pilot baselining rather than invented here. A single severe financial fact error, privacy breach, unauthorized action, or falsified evidence claim can be a blocking defect regardless of aggregate score.
