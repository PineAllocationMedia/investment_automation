## C. Target responsibilities and boundaries (proposed)

**CEO:** retains strategy, investment philosophy, protected-policy amendments,
material legal/commercial decisions, canonical merges, deployment, production and
V2 promotion. No specialist or PM may self-expand authority.

**PAM Financial Systems:** private research and portfolio intelligence first;
preserve the five V2 pillars, validated progress, evidence qualification and human
investment decisions. Future independent/consolidated portfolios are a design
goal. Financial Systems does not gain trading, production or migration authority.

**PAM Media & Communication:** Marketing & GTM owns approved editorial planning,
research, draft preparation, revisions and learning for Blossom. Preserve the
existing operation, audience-value-first objective, English public copy, fresh
source verification, factual/visual checks and affiliate transparency. CEO
publication approval remains mandatory; no new channel, paid campaign, product
claim, data right, personalized advice or commercial authority is implied.

**PM:** coordinates both activities, keeps a lightweight view of commitments,
shared resources and conflicts, and escalates actual CEO decisions. Proposed
ordinary work rule: an invoked role may choose and execute reversible steps
inside a previously approved mandate and task envelope, with compact PM
visibility at meaningful commitment, dependency or outcome changes. New authority,
cross-activity conflicts, spending and protected matters escalate before action.
This rule is a proposal; current routing remains in force until amended.

**Shared specialists:** Architecture & Audit supplies independent material-risk
assurance; Data & Evidence owns external-source truth/provenance; Data Analyst
examines accumulated internal records without rewriting them; Product & UX designs
truthful private and media surfaces; Commercial & Compliance assesses source-use,
claims and material legal/commercial risks; Codex executes bounded authorized
repository tasks. A shared role is consulted only when its expertise is needed.
Keep exactly one primary responsible role per WO; no new department heads,
permanent meetings or automatic specialist invocations are proposed.
