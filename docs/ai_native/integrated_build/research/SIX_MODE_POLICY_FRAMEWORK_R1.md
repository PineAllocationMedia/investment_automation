## Three-layer policy + bounded discretionary reasoning

1. **Universal invariants:** V2 facts and evidence cannot change with selected mode; immutable/source/PIT/rights/date/currency/cost qualifiers, no fabricated ER, no unverified portfolio data, no unauthorized financial data expenses, no automatic trading or V1/V2 promotion. Preserve existing protected method versions and restrict investment-policy updates to explicit CEO approval.
2. **Mode-specific, versioned approved contract:** the objective, eligible instruments, risk/uncertainty constraints, reference/benchmark, portfolio-room/concentration/asset bands, reserves, income/rebalancing/sales rules and evidence grades **must be CEO-approved**. Unapproved values are `UNKNOWN`. Existing four-mode V2 policies remain authoritative and unchanged in their original scope.
3. **Investor/request context:** optional concise personal profile plus explicit, editable per-analysis mandate (objective, horizon, needs, risk constraints, investable universe, account/platform, known tax/commission/FX costs, dated portfolio scope, permitted sources). The explicit prompt overrides **optional profile preferences only within approved policy**; missing decisive facts stay `UNKNOWN`. Analyst may reason and select within approved bands and explain why, but cannot silently widen policy or replace facts with subjective preference. Out-of-band recommendation = **proposal for separate human review**, not valid binding action.

**A/A/B interpretation confirmed by CEO:**
- **A — Legacy relation:** six R1 proposals are versioned separately and inherit existing V2 knowledge; V2's `balanced`, `family-office`, `rational-aggressive` and `high-cagr` are **not rewritten or activated as new R1 policies**.
- **A — Structural cash:** only `modern-adaptive-browne` may be *proposed* for an explicit standing strategic liquidity sleeve, subject to dedicated future approval. V2's universal benchmark-first/reserve-exceptional path continues **within the original four V2 modes**. Temporary settlements, payout/withdrawal buffers and confirmed near-term liabilities are **operational** cash requirements, not implied strategic reserves.
- **B — Bounded adaptability:** every approved R1 policy will define its own CEO-approved floors/ceilings or allowable ranges; judgement within those ranges is documented, not a formula automatically optimizing away constraints. **No specific numerical bands were selected in this conversation.**

## Minimum common approval checklist — unresolved across new R1 contracts

1. **Objective, horizon, permanent-loss/volatility and liquidity budget:** by mode, with account/mandate overrides allowed *only* in defined ranges.
2. **Eligible assets, geography, leverage/derivatives, account/currency rules:** by mode; no asset is investable solely because a mode name suggests it.
3. **Risk/concentration/portfolio/hedge-sleeve bands:** specify policy numeric or conditional thresholds, required confidence and override escalation; `UNKNOWN` until CEO picks.
4. **Benchmarks, opportunity cost, Expected Return and income/real-return logic:** source-consistent scenario and cost standards; do not use VFV hurdle universally.
5. **Contribution-first, rebalance/structural reserve/sale doctrine:** preserve existing V2 rules in scope, design new R1-specific exceptions and require separate CEO approval.
6. **Source/valuation/report acceptance:** source hierarchy, rights and forecast-confidence classes, market/corporate-action/currency checks, independent financial and privacy review; no real-case accepted yet.
