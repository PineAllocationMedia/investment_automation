# R1 mode policy proposal — Rational-Aggressive

**Status:** `PROPOSAL_ONLY / NOT_APPROVED / NOT_LOADED`; design draft v0.1, 2026-10-29. Not an executable model, a V2-mode mutation, an approved investment rule or a licensed source. The CEO selected the *qualitative direction* below; specific quantitative and eligibility policy remains `UNKNOWN / CEO_POLICY_GATE`. Independent material financial/evidence review and separately authorized CEO policy approval are required before decision-grade use.

**Common R1 foundations:** one CC → IR → PI → CC manual system; Quality, Durability, Expected Return, Confidence and Portfolio Room; issuer/company facts invariant to mode; provenance, PIT, source rights, risk and cost uncertainty explicit; no trade, spending, unsupported confidence upgrade, V2 promotion or unilateral method updates. Human-provided objective/horizon/account costs/currency and a short optional profile are context, not evidence. The per-analysis prompt prevails over optional profile preferences **only where consistent with approved policy and factual/safety constraints**. Discretion within approved bands must be explained and source-/mandate-linked; suggested exceptions require human policy approval, not quiet execution.

## CEO orientation (23-answer review; design selected, not financial policy approval)

- **Objective:** maximize defensible long-term **expected compound return** with strong valuation discipline, not simply chase high beta.
- **Volatility:** can be tolerated when a **sufficiently evidenced asymmetric payoff** supports it; permanent-loss and evidence uncertainty are not the same as quoted volatility.
- **Universe:** **high-quality businesses with sustainable growth**, not a default speculative/unrestricted asset menu. Company size alone is not a sufficient investment conclusion.
- **Capital:** concentrated positions only when conviction is calibrated to authentic evidence, conservative forward Expected Return and **net** brokerage/FX/tax costs. No fabricated expected alpha or investment weights.

## V2 relationship and implementation proposal

Retain existing V2 `rational-aggressive` authority. Its defensiveness/reserve propensity is conceptually below V2 `balanced` and `family-office`, above High-CAGR; concentration tolerance is ordered in reverse. The original V2 **qualified investment → benchmark → exceptional temporary reserve** doctrine remains. The R1 draft must not modify V2 concentration, hurdle or lifecycle rules; any different R1 numerical policy requires CEO authorization and its own version.

- **CC:** prompt for risk tolerance, investor horizon, eligible company scope, portfolio/account costs and evidence strength.
- **IR:** evaluate quality and durable reinvestment economics, price paid, credible terminal/per-share scenarios, counter-thesis and asymmetric losses; never infer favorable odds solely from a price decline.
- **PI:** interpret actual scoped exposures, diversified/common-factor overlap and concentration; check marginal return **after all known transaction costs**; propose new-contribution percentages only under approved constraints.
- **CC report:** five pillars, economic return ranges (not false probability-weighted Expected Return), source/valuation/confidence limits, strongest loss thesis and evidence-needed to change assessment.

**Acceptance cases (future; NOT_RUN):** high-quality company with expensive valuation; apparent asymmetry from uncertain accounting; overlapping holdings and unknown FX; high-upside weak-quality speculation; stress drawdown without thesis break; original economic facts identical across V2 modes.
**Approval boundary:** the draft is nonbinding; no risk/hurdle/weight/sale policy may be inferred or executed.
