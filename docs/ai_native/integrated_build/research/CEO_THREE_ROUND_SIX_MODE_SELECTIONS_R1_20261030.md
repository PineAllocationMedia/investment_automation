## 3. Shared target operating contract — DESIGN ONLY

1. **Discovery:** on-demand international issuer and financial-instrument search, dated source rights and identifiable cohorts. Record considered/deferred and limits of universe coverage; research candidate is not investable.
2. **Deep economic analysis:** primary dated fact identity and QUALITY/DURABILITY; normalize business-appropriate organic/per-share growth, financing, reinvestment, dilution, credit/working capital and cash, valuation and counter-thesis; separate facts, estimates, forecasts, uncertainty and arithmetic verification. 4. **Portfolio Room / destination:** PI evaluates dated scoped positions, direct/ETF overlap, account platform and costs, concentration, new-contribution percentages when supportable, plus permitted alternatives. Explicitly distinguish unavailable net cost economics or unknown holdings from a negative investment judgement. No routine sell or silent reserve.
5. **CC report:** decision-first recommendation/conditional status/no-action with reason, qualified five pillars, researched companies vs *admissible purchasable instruments*, mode-appropriate benchmark, alternatives, material blockers, evidence annex and one human next step; standalone exportable document remains an output *proposal* subject to supported capability, not a new mandatory policy or proof of a PDF. Real manual transfers are not automatic Project calls.

### Instrument translation and platform gate (Q29, Q38)

An economic issuer is **not** identical to a tradable instrument. Record original share-class/market, secondary listing, ADR or Canadian Depositary Receipt (CDR) where one actually exists, funded CAD-hedged security if genuinely documented, or eligible fund. Inspect exact legal/economic exposure, depositary/security wrapper, ratio and corporate actions, currency, FX-hedging mechanics/effectiveness, costs, bid/ask, liquidity, trading eligibility, tax/account constraints, platform support and source rights. A CDR is not an automatic duplicate of underlying shares and does not grant blanket derivatives/options/leverage authority. A local substitute with similar economics is **a different researched candidate** with separate Quality/Durability/Expected Return/Confidence checks. Do not allocate to a thesis with no actually available policy/account-eligible vehicle; display it only as research or a deferred opportunity. A platform capability must be observed or reported as UNKNOWN, never inferred.

### Portfolio evidence boundary (Q10)

Design candidate levels: **VISIBLE_SOURCE** = factual description of displayed content within observed scope; **CEO_ATTESTED_SCOPE** = user attests what selected view is intended to represent, permitting qualified conditional interpretation only; **ACCOUNT_VERIFIED** = actually verified dated account-level evidence adequate for specific monetary calculations. These proposed labels are NOT an authorization to relabel public Blossom data as private, declare cash/position completeness or derive account monetary denominators from an attestation. Reconcile with [existing PI observation contract](../portfolio/OBSERVATION_AND_ROOM_R1.md) before changing code, prompts or conclusions. Ordinary account/commission/tax/FX unknowns remain UNKNOWN, not zero.

### Benchmark and capital fallback boundary (Q03, Q04, Q30, Q37)

Mode-specific **approved set of benchmarks**, with reasoned choice on each request, is a design direction; no benchmark set, synthetic forward ETF return, statistical alpha or universal minimum return spread is approved. Under **existing High-CAGR V2 policy**, VFV remains primary opportunity-cost benchmark and normal fallback in the **qualified individual investment → benchmark VFV → exceptional temporary reserve** hierarchy; no automatic 100 % VFV purchase and no unapproved reserve. Preserve other old V2 mode policy in its original scope; non-VFV R1 references and multi-asset policy require formal approval. An unsupported investment comparison may end in a properly explained conditional/no-action rather than an invented ranking.

**Stress convention (Q33A):** 10–20 %, 15–25 % or 35–45 % are scenarios/aspirational resilience targets, not verified maximum losses, stop-loss levels, expected drawdown probabilities or automatic buy/sell triggers. Permanent impairment is assessed separately; an asset may lose materially more than a scenario. No risk budget is approved simply by selecting a stress range.
