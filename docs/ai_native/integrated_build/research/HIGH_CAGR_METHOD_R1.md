# M2 R1 — High-CAGR research method, faithful to existing V2 philosophy
**Status:** CANDIDATE reusable analyst method, **not** a new CEO-approved investment policy and **not** V2 runtime authority.  
**Primary role:** Investment Research, subject review pending. **Applies only when:** approved `high-cagr` strategy explicitly selected; 5–10-year horizon is the constitutional default intent, not a substituted user mandate.

## Policy inheritance — source, no inventions
Read [Constitution §3–4](../../../../PAM_PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md) before treating a conclusion as policy. High-CAGR Quality Compounder — Asymmetric is built on five distinct lenses:

1. **QUALITY:** recurring economics, margin/capital discipline, cash after *all* material reinvestment, leverage and per-share financing effects.
2. **DURABILITY:** evidence-backed moat, runway, disruption and structural permanent-capital-loss routes; distinguish cyclical drawdowns from thesis breaks.
3. **EXPECTED RETURN:** conservative, transparent 5–10-year economic/per-share growth + distributions + plausible valuation-normalization scenarios when data permit; no required opaque point score.
4. **CONFIDENCE:** fact confidence and forecast confidence separated; uncertainty lowers precision or conviction, it is not negative business quality.
5. **PORTFOLIO ROOM:** account/holdings-specific incremental capacity **belongs to PI**, not IR; remain `AWAITING_PI` with no portfolio evidence.

Constitutional **existing qualitative portfolio principles** include: VFV as opportunity-cost benchmark and safety core; new contributions before habitual selling; exceptional sales require thesis review; concentration and source uncertainty must be accounted for. The Constitution also expresses rough High-CAGR equity/VFV concentration and relative-return guidelines, including the conceptual VFV+~2pp established-company and +~3–4pp less-established-company hurdles; these are **not** universally executable numerical admission rules or permission to assign new portfolio target weights. Do not invent a VFV Expected Return to test the hurdle.

The V2 modes are `balanced`, `family-office`, `rational-aggressive` and `high-cagr`. The economic facts stay the same across modes; only an **actually approved** mode-specific policy can modify the decision. No universal High-CAGR default.

**Critical applicability boundary:** `src/expected_return_approved_policy_v1.py` is specifically a CEO-approved `ORDINARY_COMPOUNDER`, `USD`, `US_GAAP` proposal/version `2026-09-29-owner-approved-ordinary-nonpayer-v1`. Its 0/3/5% mature-growth and 12/16/20x terminal P/E scenario anchors are **not** automatic global/IFRS/technology/biotech defaults, and its nonpayer route is not interchangeable with a payer route. Do not apply those constants to T13's international cases unless applicability and actual approved runtime conditions are verified. V2 PR5D2/PR5E contracts have stronger qualifying prerequisites; this AI method is not an alternative admission path or synthetic proof.

## Analyst procedure — use proportionate depth
**A. Mandate:** goal, strategy/version, 5–10-year or user-given horizon, benchmark and as-of; research status `EXPLORATORY` unless decisive real-source and method conditions satisfy the declared separate gate.

**B. Economic comparison:** establish business model, competitive structure, organic vs acquired growth, financial reporting basis, per-share dilution and financing, cash after necessary reinvestment, liabilities and company-specific future risks. For cyclical, pharma, software, asset-heavy and cross-listed businesses, choose relevant economic normalizations rather than using a single growth or FCF multiple.

**C. Challenge & confidence:** articulate highest-impact fragility, competing explanations for earnings growth, sector/geopolitical drivers and invalidation conditions. Identify material missing data that actually change conclusions. Avoid speculation about short-term market regimes.

**D. Scenario readiness:** if genuinely normalized annual per-share owner economics and comparable dated class price, payout assumptions and capital changes are available, move to [VALUATION_R1.md](VALUATION_R1.md). If not, report `EXPECTED_RETURN_UNAVAILABLE` or bounded conditional ranges (never statistical expected return). Use the right benchmark and expenses when supported.

**E. IR output:** concise Research Capsule with Company / Class / As-of, Quality, Durability, Expected Return status, fact-confidence and forecast-confidence, key counter-thesis, unsupported items, original sources and next-owner questions. `Portfolio Room` always deferred to PI.
