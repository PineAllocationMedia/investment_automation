# R1 mode policy proposal — Intelligent Dividend Harvester

**Status:** `PROPOSAL_ONLY / NOT_APPROVED / NOT_LOADED`; design draft v0.1, 2026-10-29. Not an executable model, a V2-mode mutation, an approved investment rule or a licensed source. The CEO selected the *qualitative direction* below; specific quantitative and eligibility policy remains `UNKNOWN / CEO_POLICY_GATE`. Independent material financial/evidence review and separately authorized CEO policy approval are required before decision-grade use.

**Common R1 foundations:** one CC → IR → PI → CC manual system; Quality, Durability, Expected Return, Confidence and Portfolio Room; issuer/company facts invariant to mode; provenance, PIT, source rights, risk and cost uncertainty explicit; no trade, spending, unsupported confidence upgrade, V2 promotion or unilateral method updates. Human-provided objective/horizon/account costs/currency and a short optional profile are context, not evidence. The per-analysis prompt prevails over optional profile preferences **only where consistent with approved policy and factual/safety constraints**. Discretion within approved bands must be explained and source-/mandate-linked; suggested exceptions require human policy approval, not quiet execution.

## CEO orientation (23-answer review; design selected, not financial policy approval)

- **Objective:** build **growing, sustainably funded dividend income** over the long term; no automatic yield maximization or ex-dividend trading system.
- **Universe:** **dividend-paying equities, dividend ETFs and REITs/FPI**. This selection is not an authorization for bonds, covered-call products, preferred shares or derivatives in this specific mode.
- **Cash flows:** meet **explicitly documented user cash needs first**, then consider reinvesting remaining distributions; operational cash pending payment is not a standing strategic reserve allocation.
- **Opportunity selection:** an attractive indicated yield is relevant only when coverage, issuer/fund balance sheet, distribution source/quality and valuation are all adequately substantiated. Total-return erosion, payout cuts, and cost/tax drag cannot be ignored.

## R1 analytical behavior proposed after policy approval

- **CC:** identify need for spendable cash, payment frequency and currency, reinvestment option, account/tax status, horizon and portfolio source. A short profile may aid questions but cannot fabricate cash needs.
- **IR:** research organic and per-share distribution growth, retained earnings/FCF and necessary reinvestment, payout coverage, leverage, cyclical stress and return-of-capital composition. For ETFs and FPI, confirm fund/REIT cash-flow mechanics; model dividend growth **with share price/total return risk**, not dividend yield alone. Verify source rights and historical distribution dates.
- **PI:** estimate after-tax/FX/fee cash available only with supplied account facts; record whether distributions cover the user's documented need and remaining new-money opportunity; inspect exposure and correlation.
- **CC report:** sustainability and credible income-growth scenarios, risks to principal and purchasing power, net cash-flow availability, five pillars, unknown source/account costs, and one manual next step. No dividend capture arbitrage claim or automated reinvestment/order.

**Acceptance cases (future; NOT_RUN):** headline yield funded by declining FCF; FPI or ETF distribution containing return of capital; dividend growth offset by dilution or shrinking share price; paid-out needs in CAD from USD distributions with unknown taxes; reinvestment with net costs; ex-dividend price adjustment.
