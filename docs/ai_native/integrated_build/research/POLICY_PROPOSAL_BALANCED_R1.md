## Relationship to existing V2 modes — critical reconciliation

**Already documented in existing V2 policy/roadmap:** modes `balanced`, `family-office`, `rational-aggressive`, `high-cagr` share the same underlying economic facts and the same **qualified company → benchmark → exceptional temporary reserve** hierarchy. The roadmap's conceptual order is:

- Defensiveness / reserve propensity: `balanced >= family-office >= rational-aggressive >= high-cagr`.
- Concentration tolerance: `balanced <= family-office <= rational-aggressive <= high-cagr`.
- A reserve is **not** an automatic recession/volatility or market-timing response. Final numeric mode thresholds remain future contracts.

**Not established:** that the new R1 **Balanced** is the identical legal/financial policy as V2 `balanced`; that it automatically authorizes strategic bonds, cash, rebalancing bands or preset stock/bond weights; that any proposed new Balanced rule replaces V2's existing hierarchy. A standalone R1 policy must be reconciled and CEO-approved. Keep V2 runtime unchanged.

## Intended role — proposal, not operational instruction

Balanced R1 could aim for **long-term growth with diversification and resilience**, rather than High-CAGR's aggressive compound-growth orientation or pure capital preservation. The five analytical pillars retain their factual definitions; decision emphasis and portfolio capacity would use the *approved* Balanced mandate.

**Provisional design preference for CEO consideration:** Start with the existing V2 `balanced` objectives and ordering as the **comparison baseline**, then decide explicitly whether the future R1 Balanced mandate permits any additional defensive instruments/strategic allocations. Do not silently inherit High-CAGR VFV thresholds or invent a bond allocation.

## Proposed R1 specialist behavior **only after approval**

- **CC**: distinguish a request for existing V2 `balanced` mode from newly approved R1 Balanced, retain explicit version/method and investor context; fail closed on alias ambiguity.
- **IR**: compare well-sourced underlying economics and cost/risk-adjusted scenarios across permitted instruments; facts must remain invariant across strategy labels.
- **PI**: evaluate diversification/look-through and Portfolio Room from dated private observations and approved risk/concentration boundaries; only supportable contribution percentages, unknown when data are incomplete.
- **CC report**: five-pillar summary under the authorized Balanced policy, material risk diversification/correlation caveats, account FX/cost/tax limitations, what V2 `balanced` does/does not authorize, strongest countercase, manual next decision. Never imply strategy weights are optimized from labels alone.
