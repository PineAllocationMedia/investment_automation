## CEO orientation (23-answer review; design selected, not financial policy approval)

- **Objective:** integrated quality growth of investment portfolios with household/family cash needs, tax considerations and major risk resilience.
- **Scope:** **investment portfolios only**. No real estate, family legal, estate or total-balance-sheet asset coverage is presumed. Cash obligations supplied in the mandate can inform portfolio investment decisions without expanding the asset universe.
- **Risk approach:** quality compounding, greater protection from major economic impairment and credible stresses than Rational-Aggressive/High-CAGR, but not automatic recession timing.
- **Capital path:** qualified investments first; existing V2 family-office benchmark-first and exceptional temporary reserve-last policy is **preserved**, not replaced by a standing strategic cash allocation.

## What CC, IR, PI and the report would do, after CEO policy approval

- **CC:** obtain whose portfolio(s) are in scope, currency, goals, investment horizon, major obligations, contribution funding and account/tax data; no inferred household asset ownership or consolidated account access.
- **IR:** compare issuer Quality/Durability, credible economic stress and normalized return scenarios with the approved family-office opportunity cost. Distinguish liquidity and solvency facts from sentiment. Preserve V2 family-office identity in versioned method references.
- **PI:** distinguish known and missing dated portfolios/accounts; inspect concentration/look-through, correlation and account-specific net fees/FX/tax assumptions; favor percentages of NEW contributions. Do not model off-platform holdings as observed or suggest routine liquidation from macro headlines.
- **CC report:** family-investment objective versus source-supported portfolio resilience, tax/cash constraints as known or `UNKNOWN`, five pillars, costs, countercase, and human next check. No trade.
