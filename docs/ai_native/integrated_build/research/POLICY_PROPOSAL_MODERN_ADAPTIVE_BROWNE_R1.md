## CEO orientation (23-answer review; design selected, not financial policy approval)

- **Objective:** robust diversification and relatively limited drawdowns across adverse economic environments, **without guaranteeing protection**.
- **Four functional exposures retained:** growth participation, deflation/long-duration sensitivity, inflation/monetary shock resilience, and short-duration/liquidity resilience. Typical historical instruments (equities, high-quality sovereign bonds, gold and cash-like assets) are *examples for analysis*, not implicitly authorized allocations or source-certified hedges.
- **Adaptation:** instruments, geographic coverage, currency and duration may vary **within future approved bands**, supported by real economics and the person's mandate. No mandatory 25/25/25/25 weights or newly invented fixed alternative.
- **Weighting/rebalance:** adaptive to documented risk and investor needs, **not speculative prediction** of the next macro regime. Review on a disclosed interval **plus** evidence-based material change triggers; advice only, no execution.

## Different from Balanced and V2 — must be resolved before binding policy

Balanced pursues growth with stronger diversification, primarily seeking a risk/return compromise. This mode instead evaluates the **function and failure of each economic protection sleeve** through stress scenarios. A structural liquidity sleeve is **potentially admissible here only**, following separate CEO approval of permitted instruments, ranges and conditions. This is a distinct future **R1** method, *not* an exception to existing V2 mode capital-destination or cash-reserve doctrine. Ordinary transaction liquidity or specifically documented near-term withdrawal needs remain distinct from a strategic portfolio allocation to cash. The V2 four-mode reserve-last ordering and High-CAGR policy are preserved.

## Proposed CC / IR / PI / report duties only after approval

- **CC:** obtain spending currency, investor horizon, real/nominal objectives, need for liquid funds, allowable downside, inflation expectations as assumptions not facts, and precise instrument restrictions.
- **IR:** analyze each functional exposure with price, duration, credit/sovereign risk, real-yield, gold/currency and path dependence; compare authentic sources through growth/inflation/deflation/stagflation stresses **without claiming reliable macro timing**. Include scenarios where usual diversifiers fall simultaneously.
- **PI:** attribute actually observed holdings/funds to economic sleeves without double counting; inspect FX/cost and look-through unknowns; propose only conditional new-contribution/periodic rebalance options when permitted and supported by actual weights, costs and policy.
- **CC report:** show protection sleeve coverage, stresses that defeat the portfolio, real return and drawdown uncertainty, diversification caveats, current sources/rights and one human action. Never promise capital preservation or a mechanically optimized mix.
