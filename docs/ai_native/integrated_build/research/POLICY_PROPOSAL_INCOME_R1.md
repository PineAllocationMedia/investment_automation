**Status:** `PROPOSAL_ONLY / NOT_APPROVED / NOT_LOADED` (2026-10-29).
**Scope:** CEO-confirmed R1 strategy D; separate future income investment method, **not** High-CAGR policy reused under another name. **Accountable owner:** Program Manager for the decision package; independent Investment Research / Portfolio Intelligence and material-risk review before approval.
**Dependencies:** [R1 delivery plan](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md); [36-decision Blueprint](../../../strategy/PAM_FINANCIAL_SYSTEMS_AI_NATIVE_REFORM.md); Constitution and Governance (both CEO-protected); existing source, fundamental, payout, valuation and portfolio-cost contracts.

## Intended role — proposal, not operational instruction

Income strategy should evaluate **sustainable, usable net cash distributions and capital resilience over the investor's requested horizon**, not merely maximize quoted dividend yield. The common five pillars remain relevant but need income-specific treatment: Quality of funding, Durability of payouts, forward after-cost Expected Return (including distributions **and** principal), Confidence in source data/forecast and portfolio-income capacity/room.

**Provisional design preference for CEO consideration:** Prefer repeatable cash generation and distribution coverage over yield chasing, while explicitly accounting for dividend cuts, interest-rate exposure, leverage, inflation, tax and total-return erosion. This is **not approved** and cannot imply buy/sell advice.

## Proposed R1 specialist behavior **only after approval**

- **CC**: obtain explicit need and target currency, portfolio/account, distribution timing and risk constraints; never infer spending needs from an income strategy label.
- **IR**: trace distributions to issuer cash economics and fund mechanics, separate coupon vs discretionary dividend vs return of capital, evaluate debt, duration and payout stress; model net cash income *and* capital/total-return scenarios where data permit.
- **PI**: use dated authorized scoped holdings; check issuer/look-through concentration, payment currency, account-specific fees/FX/taxes and capital drawdown interactions. Favor percentages of **new** capital if supported; no portfolio sale from yield movements alone.
- **CC report**: requested income need vs supported net cash-flow range, payout sustainability/counter-thesis, possible real-income erosion, five pillars, underlying total-return risk, costs/unknowns and one next human check. Never invent a dividend growth rate or imply a payout is guaranteed.

## Target acceptance cases after implementation (NOT_RUN)

1. High dividend yield funded by worsening free cash flow or leverage — report yield-trap risk, not income-quality certification.
2. Distribution labeled return of capital vs genuine net cash earnings; show the distinction without invented tax treatment.
3. An interest-bearing holding with FX mismatch to spending currency and unknown tax status — conditional, no exact net income.
4. Different payout timing and withdrawal needs, including missing holdings coverage; report shortfall confidence accurately.
5. Strategy policy unapproved or not actually loaded — exploratory facts only, no income-specific allocation or buy recommendation.
