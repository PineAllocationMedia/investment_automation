## 4. Findings: observed defects vs interpretation vs proposed changes

### F1 — High-CAGR fallback not fully reasoned through

**Authority already in force:** the [Constitution](../../../../PAM_PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md) explicitly orders **qualified individual opportunities → VFV benchmark → exceptional temporary reserve** within its scoped High-CAGR doctrine. The [High-CAGR research method](../research/HIGH_CAGR_METHOD_R1.md) preserves this hierarchy; the [contribution method](../portfolio/CONTRIBUTIONS_AND_COSTS_R1.md) permits bounded conditional/no-action outputs.

**Observation:** IR found insufficient evidence to establish any researched stock's superiority versus VFV, and the integrated output established no allocation. **PM inference:** CC/PI did not sufficiently examine VFV as the normal eligible alternative before settling on `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. This is **not** CEO approval for automatic 100% VFV or treating VFV as risk-free. A defensible future output must show each branch's eligibility and precise source/account/cost blockers, perhaps ending with a justified conditional decision or no action.

### F2 — PI source identity and permissible descriptive inference

**Observation:** PI correctly refused to relabel a public/profile visibility record as a personal broker-verified account. **PM interpretation:** subsequent decision flow was unnecessarily binary: account-truth was missing, but visible-source descriptive evidence and conditional comparisons may still be useful.

**Proposal requiring separate method review:** distinguish `VISIBLE_SOURCE` (what screen shows), `CEO_ATTESTED_SCOPE` (user identifies selected visible scope without broker certification) and `ACCOUNT_VERIFIED` (actually checked private scoped account data). A CEO attestation may support *conditional scoped interpretation*, never transform a public display into broker-verified portfolio holdings, cash, dates or derived monetary denominators. Honor existing [PI observation](../portfolio/OBSERVATION_AND_ROOM_R1.md) restrictions; do not alter or bypass them merely by renaming a state.

### F3 — Issuer research vs portfolio investment admission

**Observation:** diverse research candidates progressed to qualitative prioritization but not comparable, authenticated, after-cost investment decisions. **PM diagnosis:** keep separate: discovery candidate → research-depth eligibility → normalized financial/valuation support → approved policy qualification → dated PI Room/account costs → conditional investment destination. A business with strong growth and complex lending/working capital economics must not be scored as investable from top-line growth alone.

**Proposal:** reuse scoped V1/V2 scoring, quality/durability, expected-return and [research participation classifier](../../../../docs/contracts/v2/capabilities/RESEARCH_PARTICIPATION_CLASSIFIER.md) only where definitions and evidence match; never convert research `FULL_ANALYSIS_ELIGIBLE` or `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` into buy/hold/sell authority. V2's approved ordinary USD/US-GAAP nonpayer policy is **not** automatically applicable to global, IFRS, cash-paying or fintech cases.

### F4 — GitHub method loading / instructions divergence

**Observation/limits:** multiple historical Project instruction files, active R1 Project Settings and linked GitHub methods coexist; specialist claims of methods loaded require their actual retrieved blob/version, not a GitHub link. The CEO reported synchronization of the latest settings before test #3, but PM cannot inspect those settings or prove universal method loading.

**Proposal, NOT APPROVED architecture replacement:** consider concise stable Project bootstrap → on-demand read of a single authoritative GitHub instruction/method/version chain → explicit access failure handling. Consolidate duplicate active-looking instructions while preserving old revisions and existing protected governance. Such changes should not silently assume native GitHub access in every ChatGPT Project.

### F5 — Executive output quality and test burden

**Observation:** final chat report existed but no independently established standalone report artifact. Human handoffs still moved large text payloads.

**Proposal, NOT an approved output mandate:** consider decision-first standalone PDF/exportable artifact when the capability exists, with concise five-pillar executive reading, fallback VFV decision branches, data/rights limits and separate evidence annex. Optimize transfer burden by keeping integrity, not removing evidence. Test active human time instead of asserting the ≤10-minute reading and ~1 human hour/week targets are achieved.
