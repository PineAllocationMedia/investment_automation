# M4 R1 — User-initiated reassessment questions and method evolution
**Owner:** Command Center, PM oversight. **Status:** `USER_INITIATED_ONLY / NO_AUTOMATIC_ANALYSIS`.

## Research questions, not background alerts
Only when the CEO initiates a specific research, company reassessment or portfolio mandate, inspect relevant previously recorded theses and the facts needed to evaluate changes. These are **possible analytical questions**, not subscribed watch conditions or autonomous triggers.

| Requested review question | Evidence to examine when asked | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Is an investment thesis still intact? | Previous thesis ID, new original filing, intervening changes | Reanalysis, not automatic sale |
| Has debt, dilution or liquidity changed? | New share/debt/financing events, periods, comparability | Reassess per-share business thesis |
| Is growth organic or acquisition-driven? | Aligned original disclosure periods and prior explanation | Update Quality/Durability if evidence changes |
| Did valuation become more or less attractive? | Comparable dated price, current facts, and previously stated assumptions | No invented alpha or forced action |
| Has sector or macro context changed the business economics? | Dated evidence linked to company cash generation | Stress context, **not market timing** |
| Does the current portfolio need a review? | Newly supplied authorized, dated holdings and account inputs | Conditional Portfolio Room; no unseen trades |

A fact is `OBSERVED` only when actually checked on the requested date/basis. Otherwise write `NOT_CHECKED`, `INACCESSIBLE` or `CONFLICTED`. Do not assume no event occurred merely because the CEO has not requested a report.

## Invocation model — zero compulsory cadence
**Sole trigger:** the CEO explicitly asks for an analysis or reassessment. The interval may be days, months, years or unspecified. No default weekly report, mandatory review date, recurring task, automated market watch, notification, follow-up execution or agent-initiated mandate is part of Financial Systems R1.

When a prior relevant analysis exists and is actually accessible, preserve its identity, original research cutoff, method and key assumptions; compare **prior state versus newly observed evidence**, then explain whether Quality, Durability, valuation, Confidence or the thesis really changed. If none exists, perform a new analysis without inventing a historical baseline. The CEO can expressly ask to summarize a specific calendar week, but the calendar is never a schedule.

The prior ~1-human-hour-per-week target is **not** a required cadence or promise of an automatic weekly session. If workload is later measured, use hours per actual user-initiated mandate.

## Governed improvements
When the CEO requests another case, a method issue can be recorded and proposed for future versioned correction. Preserve original decisions, source dates and counter-theses; do not retroactively change historical conclusions or chase realized returns. Specialist owns technical method assessment, PM coordinates, CEO retains protected policy and merge authority. Proposed refinements are never self-applied.
