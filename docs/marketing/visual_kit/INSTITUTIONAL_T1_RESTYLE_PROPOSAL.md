## What the actual 390px preview showed

- **Strength:** the statement “A price cut doesn't erase the mortgage bill” is intelligible; the evidence panel identifies `29` as a homebuilder-confidence index, and the three mechanisms are readable in sequence.
- **Visual weakness:** one large green rounded metric panel followed by **three matching rounded boxes** reads as an introductory teaching card. Serial `01/02/03` labels dominate the research voice.
- **Comprehension weakness:** the WATCH note is **visually too small**, sits in another filled bar and leaves a large underused lower band. The information is not wrong merely because the architecture feels simplistic.
- **Review distinction:** the packet flagged mobile WATCH and professional restraint `HOLD`; its own independent skeptical factual/audit step was **PENDING**. Do not repurpose this draft as a verified production sample.

## Proposed research-note layout for T1 Classic (same 1080×1350, same input/evidence)

1. **Masthead:** the exact approved pine seal, PAM and family title; subtle issue identifier and date. No copyright glyph attached to the seal. Compact, straight dividing rule.
2. **Thesis block:** strong but restrained headline and two-line explanatory deck, with clear negative space. Avoid theatrical all-caps section badges.
3. **Verified-signal row:** an aligned **large-but-not-poster metric** with label, exact units/reference period, source and observation as-of in the same visual reading lane. Use one fine horizontal separator rather than a high, rounded colored card.
4. **Mechanism strip:** three text clauses in **two or three crisp columns separated by hairline rules** with subtle directed sequence, not three floating rounded flashcards. Respect conditional causal verbs. Keep clauses legible at 390px, and a compact `why it matters` connective sentence if needed, instead of ornamental arrows.
5. **Second-order implication:** one concise blind spot / alternative explanation, slightly distinct typography. It is an interpretation, not an additional numeric fact.
6. **What would challenge it:** a **readable, differentiated WATCH line** set as a real decision-relevant observation, rather than tiny text in a high-contrast pill. Reserve enough room for the full bounded line.
7. **Footer:** the existing concise and accurate publisher name, full `AS OF` and advice line; optional *verified-owner* copyright notice only if CEO/rights review authorizes it, and never at the expense of source legibility.

**Palette:** the current approved pine, ivory, muted gray/sage and minimal amber only where analytically useful; avoid rich repeated filled blocks. **Geometry:** grid-based layout, column alignment, hairline rules, low-radius or square rectangular fields. **Typography:** publication-like hierarchy with clear distinction among thesis/metric/explanation/qualifier, never pretend to be a broker research desk.

## Allowed changes / forbidden scope

- Change **only composition, typographic scale/spacing and non-semantic separators** in a separately authorized implementation PR.
- Preserve deterministic text/evidence binding, current T1 JSON fields, as-of definitions, numeric labeling, approved logo byte hash, draft watermark, exact-caption SHA release chain and independent review gate.
- **Do not** “solve institutional” by automatically adding a chart or unsupported supporting indicators; Evidence+ remains opt-in and separately data-tested.
- **Do not** silently alter T2/T3, licensed assets, public brand position, data access, investment-advice language or operating authority. If one shared helper must change, add baseline regression tests proving unrelated templates remain stable.
