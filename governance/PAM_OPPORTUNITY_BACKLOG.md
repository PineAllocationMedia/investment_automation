# PAM Opportunity Backlog

> Ideas and opportunities, distinct from committed work.

Lifecycle:

```text
NEW
→ REVIEWED
→ PARKED / PROMOTED / REJECTED
```

Only `PROMOTED` ideas may become Work Order or roadmap candidates.

Each entry records:

- `ID`
- `TITLE`
- `SOURCE`
- `DESCRIPTION`
- `POTENTIAL VALUE`
- `ESTIMATED EFFORT`
- `DEPENDENCIES`
- `STATUS`
- `CEO DISPOSITION`
