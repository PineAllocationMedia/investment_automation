# Investment Automation — System Rules

## 🎯 Objective

Build a long-term investment model system focused on:
- Compounding in the most efficient way wealth over decades
- Optimizing to find the best risk-adjusted returns
- Discipline-based investing into the best positions right now for a long term high cagr purpose
- Automation of high quality and institutional grade analysis and backtesting

---

## 🧠 Core Philosophy

- Long-term investing only (10–50 years horizon)
- Avoid emotional decision-making
- Prefer robustness over optimization
- Focus on quality businesses with durable cash flows
- Reinvest dividends systematically
- Seeking the best composition to fit best chosen mode
- Become a better option than S&P 500 as a primary go-to saving investment
- Making premium active Hedge-fund like investment management accessible and possible for everybody willing to take their own finance's gestion in their hands
---

## 📊 Allowed Strategies (Blend of these)

- DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging)
- Dividend growth investing
- Factor-based investing (value, quality, low volatility)
- Basic portfolio rebalancing

---

## 🚫 Forbidden Practices

- Market timing based on short-term predictions
- Overfitting backtests to historical data
- Excessive leverage
- Strategy changes without documented reasoning

---

## 🇨🇦 Fiscal Context (VERY IMPORTANT)

The system is designed for a Canadian investor based in Quebec.

All investment decisions must consider:

### Accounts
- TFSA (CELI): tax-free growth and dividends
- RRSP: tax-deferred
- Taxable account: taxable dividends and capital gains

### Dividend considerations
- Canadian dividends are tax-efficient in taxable accounts (dividend tax credit)
- US dividends may face withholding tax (even inside TFSA)
- International diversification is still required

### Rules
- Do NOT assume US-centric tax logic
- Always evaluate after-tax returns, not just gross returns
- Prefer tax-efficient asset placement when possible

## 🧪 Validation Rules

Any new strategy must:
1. Be backtestable
2. Be reproducible
3. Be documented in DECISIONS.md
4. Be stress-tested across different market regimes
